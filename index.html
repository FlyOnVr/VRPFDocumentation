Version 1.0

Physics VR for Unity.

VR Physics Framework gives you a physics player rig, physics hands, grabbing with smooth hand posing, sockets, guns with HurricaneVR-style reloading, and full body IK. It runs on XR Plug-in Management and OpenXR alone.

PR

A rig that doesn't bounce

The body follows your real-world walking, walls push your head back, and movement accelerates and glues to slopes like Marrow.
GR

Grabbing that behaves

Light objects fly into your hand, heavy ones pull you to them. Held objects never collide with your body, so no more flying on grab.
HP

Smooth hand posing

Pose a ghost hand on any object. Hands blend in and out, support custom hand rigs, left hand poses, and mirroring (WIP).
GN

Guns with real reloading

Chamber, magazines that slide into the well, slide lock, eject and slide release buttons, charging handles, casings, recoil.
SK

Sockets

Holsters, magazine wells, suppressors and key slots, with slide-in animation and an open-well approach zone.
IK

Full body IK

Drop in any humanoid and click Auto Set. Built on Unity's own humanoid IK with procedural legs.

Up and running in four steps

  1. Import the framework into a Unity 6 URP project.
  2. VR Physics Framework › Setup Tool › Run Full Setup: layers, physics, OpenXR and interaction profiles.
  3. VR Physics Framework › Create › VRPF Player.
  4. Select a prop and click Make Selected Grabbable. Press Play, with or without a headset.
