Getting Started

From an empty scene to grabbing, posing and shooting in a few minutes. Do the installation first.

Tech demo

Open VR Physics Framework/TechDemo/Assets/Scenes/TechDemo.unity to see everything working: a table with the Makarov pistol (with a suppressor you can screw on), the AK-47 and the new SMG, spare magazines, the posed knife, a socket pad, a heavy crate, and a shooting range with damageable targets.

1. Add the player

  1. Make sure your floor has a collider.
  2. VR Physics Framework › Create › VRPF Player. It's placed at the Scene view's center, on the ground.
  3. Delete any other Main Camera in the scene.

The player is a Physics Rig with movement, a tracked head and controllers, and two physics hands with grabbers. Want your own hands? Use the Setup Tool's Player tab.

2. Make something grabbable

  1. Select a prop (any mesh).
  2. VR Physics Framework › Create › Make Selected Grabbable.

That adds a Rigidbody, a convex collider if it had none, a VRPF Grabbable and the Grabbable layer. Your hands can now grab it anywhere and throw it.

3. Add a hand pose

  1. Select the grabbable, open Setup Tool › Grabbable, tick Add right hand pose and click Add Grab Point.
  2. Move the Grab Point (with the ghost hand under it) to where the hand should hold the object.
  3. Rotate the ghost's finger bones to wrap them around the object.
  4. Optional: tick Add left hand pose too, or try Mirror Right → Left on the Posing tab (WIP).

When you grab near that point, the hand snaps to the ghost's position and the fingers blend into its pose. See Grabbing & Posing.

4. Add a gun

Drag a prefab from TechDemo/Assets/Interactables/Gun Examples/Prefabs into the scene, together with a few of its magazines. To turn your own model into a gun, use Setup Tool › Guns: see Guns & Reloading.

5. Test it

Press Play with a headset (Quest Link / Air Link / SteamVR with OpenXR), or without one using the Device Simulator.
