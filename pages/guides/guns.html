Guns & Reloading

Guns were rebuilt for 1.0 with HurricaneVR-style handling. The old gun scripts didn't fire; these are physical, chambered and reloadable.

Controls

ActionHow
FireTrigger on the hand holding the grip
Eject magazineA / X on the gun hand
Insert magazineBring it to the bottom of the mag well. It's taken from your hand and slides in.
Slide releaseB / Y on the gun hand
RackGrab the slide / charging handle with your other hand, pull back, let go
Fire modeClick the stick on the gun hand (AK, SMG)
Two handsGrab the gun anywhere with your other hand to steady it (less recoil)

The reload loop

Fire until empty
→
Slide locks back
→
A / X: mag drops
→
New mag slides in
→
B / Y or rack
→
Chambered
  • A gun fires the round in the chamber, then chambers the next one from the magazine.
  • When nothing can be chambered the slide locks back (pistols). The AK and SMG don't lock.
  • Racking with a round chambered ejects it unfired. Racking with an empty magazine locks the slide.
  • Grabbing a locked pistol slide takes it off the slide stop: let go and it slams forward ("slingshot").
  • Optional: Auto Release Slide On Mag Insert chambers as soon as a loaded mag goes in.

Parts of a gun

Makarov Pistol       Rigidbody, VRPF Grabbable, VRPF Gun, AudioSource
├─ Model             meshes + convex colliders; Slide & Trigger have VRPF Gun Part
├─ Grip              VRPF Grab Point (no ghost: fingers follow the controller)
├─ Muzzle            bullets and the muzzle flash
├─ Magazine Well     VRPF Magazine Socket
├─ Charging Handle   VRPF Grabbable (Hand Follows Object), Requires Parent Grab, VRPF Charging Handle
├─ Ejection Port     VRPF Ejector (casings)
├─ Round Ejector     VRPF Ejector (unfired rounds)
└─ Suppressor Mount  VRPF Socket accepting "Suppressor"

Magazines are their own prefabs: VRPF Magazine + VRPF Socketable (Socket Type = ammo type) + VRPF Grabbable.

Build your own

  1. Drag your gun model into the scene. Its magazine, slide/bolt and trigger should be separate child objects.
  2. Open Setup Tool › Guns, assign the model and its parts, pick the barrel axis, ammo type and capacity, fire modes and sounds.
  3. Click Build Gun. A gun prefab and a magazine prefab are saved in Gun Examples/Prefabs.
  4. Check the Grip position: its forward should point down the barrel, and the palm sits at its center. Use a Right_Poser ghost as a guide, then delete it or keep it as a pose.
  5. Move the Magazine Well to the seated magazine position and set its Entry Offset so magazines come from below the grip.
  6. Move the Charging Handle collider to where you grab the slide/handle and set its pull direction and travel.
Building from code
The example guns are built by VRPFExampleContent.cs with VRPFGunBuilder: a good reference for exact grip, muzzle and magazine positions.

Tuning

  • Recoil: backward/upward impulse and torque push the gun; hand kick back/up move the hand target. Two-handed multiplier reduces both.
  • Fire rate: Rounds Per Minute is exact even at low frame rates. Burst count for burst mode.
  • Ballistics: damage, hit force, range, pellets and spread for shotguns, impact prefab, tracers.

Debugging

Turn on Debug Logging in VRPF Settings. Every magazine insert, chamber, slide lock and dry fire is logged with the reason. The gun's inspector also shows its live state and has Fire / Eject / Rack / Release buttons in play mode.

C#
var gun = GetComponent<VRPFGun>();
gun.Fired += g => ammoCounter.text = g.RoundsLeft.ToString();
gun.onSlideLocked.AddListener(() => Debug.Log("Empty!"));
