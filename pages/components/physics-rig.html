Player · Component

Physics Rig

The player: a capsule Rigidbody body that follows your real-world walking, keeps the headset above it, and gives the physics hands their targets.

WhereVR Physics Framework › Create › VRPF Player
ScriptFramework/Player/PhysicsRig.cs
ClassVRPhysicsFramework.PhysicsRig

Overview

The body is the authority for where the player is. Real-world walking moves your head inside the tracking space; the body follows your neck with velocity, so walls stop it. If the body is blocked, the tracking space is pushed back instead, so your head can't walk through walls.

Thumbstick movement (Physics Based Movement) moves the body and the tracking space together. Nothing is jointed to the body, which is why the 1.0 rig doesn't bounce and why grabbing things can't launch you any more.

The capsule's height follows your head (crouch to fit under things). It shrinks instantly and grows slowly, checking for headroom first.

The tracking origin is interpolated with the body, and controller targets are predicted to the end of each physics step, so hands don't lag while you walk.

How to use

  1. Run VR Physics Framework › Setup Tool › Project › Run Full Setup once.
  2. Create a player with VR Physics Framework › Create › VRPF Player, or drag in TechDemo/Assets/Rigs/Physical Rig/VRPF Player.prefab.
  3. Place the player's root on the floor. Don't move the Tracking Space yourself; use Teleport() to move the player.
C#
var rig = PhysicsRig.Main;
rig.Teleport(spawnPoint.position, spawnPoint.eulerAngles.y);
rig.Turn(45f, snap: true);
Vector3 head = rig.HeadPosition;      // physics-step head position
Pose hand = rig.GetControllerPose(VRPFHand.Right, endOfStep: false);
