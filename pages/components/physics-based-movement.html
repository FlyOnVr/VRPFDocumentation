Physics Based Movement

Smooth (Marrow-style) thumbstick locomotion, smooth or snap turning, and button or arm-swing jumping for the Physics Rig.

WhereAdd Component › VR Physics Framework › Player › Physics Based Movement
ScriptFramework/Player/PhysicsBasedMovement.cs
ClassVRPhysicsFramework.PhysicsBasedMovement

Overview

Ported from 0.2. It now reads VRPF Input instead of Input System actions, and the body is moved with acceleration and deceleration instead of being teleported with MovePosition.

While grounded the body is glued to the ground: it moves along slopes without sliding, snaps down small drops, and climbs steps up to Step Height. Gravity only applies in the air.

Jumping defaults to clicking the turn stick, so it never clashes with the gun buttons (A/X eject, B/Y slide release). Jump With Hand switches to holding the button and swinging your arms up.

How to use

  1. Put it on the same object as the Physics Rig (the VRPF Player already has it).
  2. Pick which hand moves and which turns, the turn mode, and the speeds.
  3. Leave Ground Layer on Everything; the player's own layers are always ignored.
