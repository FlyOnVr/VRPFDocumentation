VRPF Grabber

Lets a physics hand grab things: finds the closest grabbable or grab point around the palm, lines the hand up, blends the pose and locks the object to the hand.

WhereAdd Component › VR Physics Framework › Interaction › VRPF Grabber
ScriptFramework/Interaction/VRPFGrabber.cs
ClassVRPhysicsFramework.VRPFGrabber

Overview

Every physics step it searches around the Palm for grabbables and grab points in range and picks the best one (grab points and higher priorities win).

On grab it lines everything up over the grabbable's Pull Time, then creates a locked joint (or, for Hand Follows Object, makes the hand follow the object). Ghost hand poses blend in through the Hand Poser.

If you press grip slightly before touching an object it still grabs (Grab Buffer Time).

While both hands hold the same rigidbody (a gun and its slide or foregrip) the hands stop colliding with each other.

How to use

  1. Put it on each physics hand next to Hand Presence Physics (the Setup Tool does this).
  2. The Palm should sit at the center of the closed hand (the grip bone on the default hands). Grab points without a pose line up with it.
  3. Assign the hand model's Hand Bone Data and Hand Poser (found automatically).
