VRPF Tracked Pose

Drives a transform from the headset or a controller using XR Plug-in Management. Replaces Tracked Pose Driver.

WhereAdd Component › VR Physics Framework › Player › VRPF Tracked Pose
ScriptFramework/Player/VRPFTrackedPose.cs
ClassVRPhysicsFramework.VRPFTrackedPose

Overview

Reads the device every Update and again right before rendering (lowest latency). On a camera it also turns off Unity's automatic camera tracking so nothing moves it twice.

Controllers use the grip pose (devicePosition / deviceRotation). The head uses the center eye.

How to use

  1. Put it on the Head (camera) and on the Left/Right Controller objects under the rig's Tracking Space.
  2. Use Position/Rotation Offset to adjust a specific controller if needed.
