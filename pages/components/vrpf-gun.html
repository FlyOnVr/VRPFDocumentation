VRPF Gun

A physical gun with HurricaneVR-style handling: chamber, magazines, slide/charging handle, slide lock, eject and slide release buttons, ejection, hitscan bullets, recoil and sounds.

WhereSetup Tool › Guns › Build Gun
ScriptFramework/Weapons/VRPFGun.cs
ClassVRPhysicsFramework.VRPFGun

Overview

Reload loop: press A/X (Eject Magazine Button) on the gun hand → the magazine drops. Bring a new magazine to the bottom of the mag well → it's taken out of your hand and slides in. If the slide is locked back, press B/Y (Slide Release Button) or pull the slide/charging handle → a round is chambered → fire.

Firing needs a chambered round. After each shot the gun chambers the next round from the magazine; when it can't, the slide locks back (if Lock Slide When Empty is on). Racking with a round in the chamber ejects it unfired; racking an empty magazine locks the slide.

Fire modes: Semi, Burst and Auto, with an optional fire selector button. Rounds Per Minute is exact even when the frame rate is lower.

Bullets are hitscan raycasts that skip the gun and the player, damage anything implementing IVRPFDamageable (like Damageable), push rigidbodies, and leave a bullet hole and tracer.

Recoil pushes the gun physically and kicks the holding hand, which springs back. Two-handed holds reduce it.

The gun's mass properties are locked on Start so moving slides and handles don't make it jitter in the hand.

How to use

  1. Fastest: open the Setup Tool's Guns tab, drag your gun model into the scene, assign its magazine, slide, handle and trigger parts and press Build Gun. It creates the gun prefab and a magazine prefab.
  2. Or start from an example in TechDemo/Assets/Interactables/Gun Examples/Prefabs (Makarov Pistol with suppressor, AK-47, SMG).
  3. Adjust the Grip, Muzzle, Magazine Well, Charging Handle and Ejection Port children.
  4. Turn on Debug Logging in VRPF Settings to see why a gun doesn't fire ("dry fire: no round chambered").
C#
gun.Fired += g => ammoText.text = g.RoundsLeft.ToString();
gun.Hit += (g, hit) => Debug.Log("Hit " + hit.collider.name);

gun.TryFire();          // pull the trigger once
gun.EjectMagazine();
gun.ReleaseSlide();
