VRPF Gun Part

A moving gun part (slide, bolt, hammer, trigger) that moves between its rest pose and a "back" pose.

WhereAdd Component › VR Physics Framework › Weapons › VRPF Gun Part
ScriptFramework/Weapons/VRPFGunPart.cs
ClassVRPhysicsFramework.VRPFGunPart

Overview

The gun cycles it on each shot and locks it back on empty; a charging handle or the trigger can drive it directly through ExternalAmount. It always shows the furthest of all three.

How to use

  1. Put it on the moving mesh. Set Back Offset (slides/bolts) or Back Rotation (triggers/hammers).
  2. Assign it to the gun's Slide or Trigger Part, or the handle's Linked Part.

Settings

SettingType / defaultWhat it does
Back OffsetbackOffsetVector3Local position offset at full travel (in the parent's space).
Back RotationbackRotationVector3= zeroLocal rotation at full travel (degrees), for hammers and triggers.
Cycle TimecycleTimefloat= 0.07Seconds for one shot cycle (back and forward again).
Lock TimelockTimefloat= 0.04Seconds to move into or out of the locked-back position.

Scripting

Cycle()Play one shot cycle.
SetLocked(bool)Hold it back.
ExternalAmount0..1 drive from another component.
CaptureRest()Use the current pose as rest.
