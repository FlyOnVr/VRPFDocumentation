Hand Presence Physics

A physics hand: a Rigidbody that chases its controller with velocity, collides with the world, and carries whatever it holds.

WhereAdd Component › VR Physics Framework › Player › Hand Presence Physics
ScriptFramework/Player/HandPresencePhysics.cs
ClassVRPhysicsFramework.HandPresencePhysics

Overview

Ported from 0.2 and rewritten for 1.0 ("Smoother Hands", "Made The Hands Respond Better"). Targets are predicted to the end of each physics step, so the hand doesn't trail behind while walking or turning.

The hand's center of mass and inertia are fixed. Animated finger colliders would otherwise change them every frame, which made held guns jitter.

Heavy objects slow the hand down (Simulate Weight). If the hand gets stuck far from the controller (in a wall) it lets go and snaps back.

Guns kick the hand target with AddRecoil; a spring returns it.

How to use

  1. Built automatically by the Setup Tool on the Left Hand / Right Hand objects, together with a VRPF Grabber.
  2. The hand model goes under it as a child. Its colliders should be on the hand's layer (Left/Right Hand Physics).
